4. Requirements
A sender MUST include a Message-ID field in every message.
A sender MUST NOT send messages without a valid signature.
A receiver SHOULD verify the signature when present.
An implementation MAY ignore unknown extensions for forward compatibility.
The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "MAY", and "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in RFC 2119.
